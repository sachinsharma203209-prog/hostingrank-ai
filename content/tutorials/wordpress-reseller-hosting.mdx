## What is reseller WordPress hosting?

Reseller hosting is a **bulk hosting account you split into smaller plans and sell**. You buy server resources wholesale from a host (Hostinger, A2 Hosting, and others offer reseller tiers), then create customer accounts under your own brand, with your own pricing.

How it works:

1. Buy a reseller plan (a big shared/VPS account with a management panel)
2. Create customer accounts — each gets their own cPanel or panel access
3. Set your own prices and package limits
4. You own billing, support, and the customer relationship
5. The upstream host handles the actual server

Margins vary wildly: wholesale WordPress hosting costs $10–40/mo per reseller account, and you can sell 5–20 customer plans on top of it.

## Is reseller WordPress hosting a good business?

**Pros:**
- Recurring revenue — hosting is billed monthly/yearly
- Low startup cost — a reseller plan costs less than a coffee per month
- Existing demand — every client site needs hosting
- You control pricing and branding

**Cons:**
- **Support is on you** — the upstream host won't talk to your customers
- **Thin margins** at the cheap end of the market
- You're competing with $3/mo shared hosting — sell on service, not price
- Technical issues (server outages) are outside your control

The realistic play: reseller hosting as **an add-on for your agency or freelance web design business** — not a standalone startup.

## How to price reseller hosting

A common formula for a single client site:

- **Your cost:** ~$1–3/site on a reseller plan
- **Your price:** $10–25/mo per site (managed updates + support)
- **Add-ons:** $5–15/mo for email, $5/mo for backups, $20–50 one-time setup

Bundle hosting into your web design packages and it becomes the highest-margin part of the deal. See [WordPress hosting and maintenance](/tutorials/wordpress-hosting-maintenance) for what to include.

## Frequently asked questions

**How much does reseller WordPress hosting cost?**
Wholesale plans run $10–40/mo depending on resources, letting you host 5–20+ client sites. Margins are healthy when you bundle support.

**Is reseller hosting profitable?**
Yes, as an add-on to web design or agency work — $10–25/mo per site with ~80% margins. Less so as a standalone product competing with $3/mo hosts.

**What is the difference between reseller and VPS hosting?**
A VPS is one big server you manage; reseller hosting is a management layer for splitting a server into sellable accounts. Resellers typically buy VPS resources and split them.

**Do I need cPanel for reseller hosting?**
Most reseller plans (Hostinger, A2) include WHM/cPanel-style account management. Cloudways uses a different model — white-label managed servers.

**What is the best reseller hosting for beginners?**
Hostinger's reseller plans — cheapest, easiest, with hPanel management. See [WordPress hosting providers](/tutorials/wordpress-hosting-providers) for the full provider landscape.
