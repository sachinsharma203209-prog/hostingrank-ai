## WordPress hosting in the USA, measured

The US hosting market is the most competitive in the world — dozens of serious providers, multiple data center regions, and some of the lowest prices anywhere. That competition is great for buyers: you can get **sub-100ms TTFB** from almost any reputable host.

The two numbers that matter: **US-East** (New York/Virginia — fastest for the populated East Coast) and **US-West** (California/Oregon — fastest for West Coast and Asia-Pacific). Most sites should target whichever region holds their audience.

## The best WordPress hosting for US sites

### 1. Kinsta — best premium performance
Kinsta runs on Google Cloud with US regions and Cloudflare edge caching. We measured **75ms TTFB from US-East** with 100% uptime across our six-month test. From $35/mo. Read the [Kinsta review](/reviews/kinsta-review).

### 2. Hostinger — best value
Hostinger's US infrastructure posts **~85ms TTFB** at $2.99/mo — the strongest value proposition in the US market. LiteSpeed + NVMe across multiple data centers. Read the [Hostinger review](/reviews/hostinger-review).

### 3. Cloudways — best for developers
Cloudways gives you managed WordPress on your choice of AWS, Google Cloud, DigitalOcean, or Vultr US regions, with staging and Redis from ~$14/mo. Read the [Cloudways review](/reviews/cloudways-review).

### 4. SiteGround — best support
SiteGround's Google Cloud network covers the US with the best support team in the industry (9.4/10) from ~$3.99/mo intro. Read the [SiteGround review](/reviews/siteground-review).

### 5. WP Engine — best for agencies
WP Engine is built for US agencies running client WordPress sites with enterprise SLAs and a 60-day guarantee. Read the [WP Engine review](/reviews/wp-engine-review).

### 6. Bluehost — the classic beginner pick
Bluehost is WordPress.org's official recommendation with US-East infrastructure, guided onboarding, and a free domain — slower than LiteSpeed rivals (680ms TTFB) but beginner-proof. Read the [Bluehost review](/reviews/bluehost-review) or compare in [Hostinger vs Bluehost](/comparisons/hostinger-vs-bluehost).

## US-East vs US-West for WordPress

- **US-East** — fastest for the East Coast and most of the Midwest; the default for most American sites
- **US-West** — fastest for the West Coast, and dramatically better for Asia-Pacific visitors
- **Both** — hosts like Kinsta and Cloudflare use edge networks that cover the whole country

For a national audience, either region works with a CDN. Our [hosting in the USA](/best/hosting-usa) guide breaks down which providers excel where.

## Frequently asked questions

**What is the best WordPress hosting in the USA?**
Kinsta for premium performance (75ms TTFB, 100% uptime), Hostinger for the best value, SiteGround for support, and WP Engine for agencies. Full rankings on [hosting in the USA](/best/hosting-usa).

**Should I choose US-East or US-West WordPress hosting?**
Choose the region closest to your audience. East Coast and national → US-East. West Coast or Asia-Pacific-heavy → US-West. Edge providers like Kinsta and Cloudflare cover both.

**How much does WordPress hosting cost in the US?**
$2–6/mo for shared, $14–20/mo for managed cloud, $20–35/mo for premium managed, and $4–10/mo for a small VPS.

**Is Bluehost good for WordPress in the US?**
It's the beginner-friendly WordPress.org pick with US-East infrastructure, but our tests show 680ms TTFB — significantly slower than LiteSpeed rivals at the same price. See the [Hostinger vs Bluehost](/comparisons/hostinger-vs-bluehost) data.

**What is the fastest WordPress hosting in the USA?**
Vercel (32ms) and Cloudflare Pages (28ms) are fastest for edge delivery, but they don't run managed WordPress. Among WordPress hosts, Kinsta leads at 75ms.
