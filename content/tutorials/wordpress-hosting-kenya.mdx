## WordPress hosting in Kenya, measured

Kenya has one of Africa's strongest internet economies — Nairobi is well connected by multiple subsea cables. The fastest WordPress hosting for Kenyan audiences uses **South Africa, EU, or Middle East infrastructure** with a CDN, keeping TTFB around **120–180ms**; US-only servers push it past 300ms.

Two things matter beyond raw speed for Kenyan buyers: **M-Pesa payment** (the default payment method for most Kenyans) and **data residency** for businesses with compliance needs.

## What Kenyan sites should look for

- **M-Pesa / Lipa Na M-Pesa** payment support
- **KES or USD billing** — compare total cost with fees
- **South Africa / EU server** with a CDN for speed
- **Local support** and data residency where needed
