## What a small business site needs

A small business website is not a hobby blog. It's a sales tool — it needs to load fast, never be down when customers visit, look professional, and take payments if you sell online. The good news: that costs far less than most owners expect.

The four things that matter:

1. **Speed** — a sub-300ms TTFB keeps visitors (and Google) happy
2. **Reliability** — 99.9%+ uptime so your site is there when customers look
3. **A professional email** — you@yourbusiness.com (see [email setup](/tutorials/wordpress-email-setup))
4. **Room to grow** — enough CPU/RAM for a store or a traffic spike later

## How much should small business hosting cost?

| Budget | What you get | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Under $5/mo | Solid shared hosting, 1 site | Hostinger Premium |
| $5–10/mo | More resources, daily backups | SiteGround GrowBig, Hostinger Business |
| $10–25/mo | Managed convenience, staging | Cloudways, WP Engine entry |
| $35+/mo | Premium managed + SLA | Kinsta |

Realistically, a **small business should spend $3–8/mo** on shared hosting, and step up to managed only when the site starts driving real revenue.

## What to buy checklist

- **SSL free** — standard everywhere, non-negotiable for trust and SEO
- **Daily backups** — automatic, because your data is the business
- **Free email or cheap add-on** — several business mailboxes
- **One-click WordPress install** — you shouldn't need to touch code
- **Renewal price known upfront** — intro prices are cheap; renewals are the real cost. See [cheap WordPress hosting](/tutorials/cheap-wordpress-hosting) for the renewal trap breakdown

## Small business common mistakes

- **Cheapest plan + domain for 5 years** — lock-in before you know the host (see [WordPress hosting and domains](/tutorials/wordpress-hosting-and-domain))
- **No backups configured** — most small sites have zero backups until disaster. [Backups guide](/tutorials/wordpress-backups)
- **Ignoring security** — small sites are the most hacked because they're assumed unguarded. [Security checklist](/tutorials/wordpress-hosting-security)
- **Skipping a professional email** — free Gmail on business cards looks unprofessional

## Frequently asked questions

**What is the best cheap WordPress hosting for a small business?**
Hostinger — 142ms TTFB, free SSL, and email at $2.99/mo. SiteGround is the support-first alternative. Both are in the [best WordPress hosting](/best/wordpress-hosting) rankings.

**How much does small business hosting cost per month?**
$3–8/mo on shared hosting is the sweet spot. Managed plans run $14–35/mo when the site becomes a revenue channel.

**Does a small business need managed WordPress hosting?**
Only once the site generates meaningful revenue. Before that, shared hosting from a quality provider does everything a business site needs.

**Should I buy hosting and domain together?**
Convenient, but separate them — keeping the domain at its own registrar means you never lose it if you change hosts. [Full guide](/tutorials/wordpress-hosting-and-domain).

**Can I start with free hosting?**
For a serious business site, no — free tiers come with ads, no custom domain, and weak performance. If budget is tight, $3/mo shared beats any free tier. See [free WordPress hosting](/tutorials/free-wordpress-hosting) for the honest breakdown.
