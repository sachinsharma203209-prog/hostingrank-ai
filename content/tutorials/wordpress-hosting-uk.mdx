## WordPress hosting in the UK, measured

London is one of the best-connected internet hubs on the planet, so UK WordPress hosting can be genuinely fast — if your host has a London data center. In our tests, providers with UK or EU infrastructure post **40–60ms TTFB** for London visitors, versus 100–150ms from US servers.

The other two things that matter for UK buyers: **GDPR-friendly data residency** and **GBP billing** so you're not paying forex fees. The best WordPress hosting in the UK delivers all three.

## The best WordPress hosting for UK sites

### 1. Kinsta — best premium performance
Kinsta runs on Google Cloud with a London region and **Cloudflare edge caching** included. Our tests measured **92ms TTFB from London** with 100% uptime and support that answers in under two minutes. From $35/mo. Read the [Kinsta review](/reviews/kinsta-review).

### 2. Cloudways — best for growing sites
Cloudways lets you pick a **London or EU server** across AWS, Google Cloud, or DigitalOcean, with managed WordPress on top — staging, Redis, and scaling included. From ~$14/mo. Read the [Cloudways review](/reviews/cloudways-review).

### 3. SiteGround — best value with UK support
SiteGround serves UK customers exceptionally well — Google Cloud infrastructure, **9.4/10 support**, and GBP billing — from ~£3/mo intro. It's the friendliest managed-feel option for a single site. Read the [SiteGround review](/reviews/siteground-review).

### 4. Vultr — best cheap VPS for developers
Vultr operates a **London data center** with high-frequency instances from ~$2.50/mo — the cheapest route to a UK-hosted WordPress if you can admin a server. Read the [Vultr review](/reviews/vultr-review).

### 5. Hostinger — best overall value
Hostinger's UK/EU infrastructure delivers **~120ms TTFB to London** from its closest nodes at $2.99/mo — hard to beat for budget sites. Read the [Hostinger review](/reviews/hostinger-review).

- **GDPR compliance** is simpler when data stays in the UK/EU

## Frequently asked questions

**What is the best WordPress hosting in the UK?**
Kinsta for premium performance (92ms London TTFB), SiteGround for the best value with top-tier support, and Hostinger for budget sites. Vultr is the developer pick with a real London data center.

**Do I need UK-based WordPress hosting servers?**
For a UK-only audience, yes — a London or EU server keeps TTFB under 60ms. If you also serve US or global visitors, a CDN matters more than the server location.

**Is WordPress hosting more expensive in the UK?**
No. Entry shared plans cost roughly £2–4/mo, managed WordPress from ~£25/mo. All the providers we recommend bill in GBP or offer it.

**Is UK WordPress hosting GDPR compliant?**
Most reputable hosts with UK/EU data centers are GDPR-ready. Check where your data residency is before buying — our [hosting in the UK](/best/hosting-uk) page flags it for each provider.

**What is the best cheap WordPress hosting in the UK?**
Hostinger is our value pick, with SiteGround close behind for the managed-feel experience. See [cheap WordPress hosting](/tutorials/cheap-wordpress-hosting) for the full budget breakdown.
