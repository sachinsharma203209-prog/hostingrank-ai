## WordPress on DigitalOcean, the developer route

DigitalOcean is the developer favorite for WordPress hosting. Its **droplets** (VPS instances) give you full control with transparent pricing — **$4/mo to start**, and our tests measured **88ms TTFB** from US-East. That's faster than most managed hosts, because you get the whole machine to yourself.

The tradeoff: you're the sysadmin. Updates, security, backups, and optimization are on you — or you use Cloudways' managed layer on top of DigitalOcean (see the [Cloudways review](/reviews/cloudways-review)).

- **Starting price:** $4/mo
- **Speed:** 88ms TTFB (US-East)
- **Uptime:** 99.99% in our tests
- **Control:** full root access
- **Billing:** hourly or monthly, no contracts

Read the full [DigitalOcean review](/reviews/digitalocean-review).

## The fastest way: one-click WordPress droplet

DigitalOcean has a **one-click WordPress image** that skips most of the setup:

1. Create an account and open the **Droplets** panel
2. Choose **WordPress** from the Marketplace images
3. Pick a region (use one near your audience), a plan ($6/mo for 1GB is a good start), and a name
4. Click **Create** — your droplet is live in under a minute
5. Wait for the build email, then visit your IP — the installer asks for your domain and admin details
6. Point DNS at the droplet IP and run `certbot` for free SSL

That's a complete WordPress install on real VPS hardware, no command line required.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is DigitalOcean good for WordPress?**
For developers, yes — 88ms TTFB at $6/mo with full control is unbeatable raw value. Non-technical users should use managed hosting or Cloudways instead.

**Can I host WordPress on DigitalOcean for free?**
There's no free tier, but a $6/mo droplet is cheaper than most shared hosting. DigitalOcean occasionally runs trial credit promotions.

**Is WordPress hosting on DigitalOcean hard?**
The one-click image is easy. Managing updates, security, and backups yourself is the real effort — that's what the extra money on managed hosts buys you.

**DigitalOcean or Vultr for WordPress?**
Both are excellent — Vultr offers slightly more value per dollar and more regions; DigitalOcean has better docs and a friendlier panel. Full data in the [DigitalOcean vs Vultr](/comparisons/digitalocean-vs-vultr) comparison.

**How do I migrate WordPress to DigitalOcean?**
Use a migration plugin (All-in-One WP Migration) or DigitalOcean's droplet import. Our [move WordPress to a new host](/tutorials/move-wordpress-to-new-host) guide covers the general process.
