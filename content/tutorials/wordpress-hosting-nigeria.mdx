## WordPress hosting in Nigeria, measured

Nigeria has Africa's largest online population, and its sites are served from **Lagos and UK/EU data centers** via multiple subsea cables. The fastest WordPress hosting for Nigerian audiences keeps TTFB around **120–180ms**; US-only servers sit above 300ms.

Two factors matter beyond speed: **Naira-friendly payment** (international cards can be a pain in Nigeria, so PayPal or providers with flexible options win) and **UK/EU data residency** for businesses that want GDPR-adjacent hosting.

## The best WordPress hosting for Nigeria

### 1. Hostinger — best overall
Hostinger reaches Nigeria at ~**150ms TTFB** from EU/Middle East nodes at $2.99/mo, with a one-click WordPress installer and free SSL. It's the most reliable budget option for Nigerian users. Read the [Hostinger review](/reviews/hostinger-review).

### 2. Cloudways — best managed for growth
Cloudways deploys managed WordPress on **UK/EU or South Africa regions** with staging, Redis, and scaling from ~$14/mo — ideal for WooCommerce and growing businesses. Read the [Cloudways review](/reviews/cloudways-review).

### 3. Kinsta — best premium
Kinsta's Cloudflare edge network delivers strong Nigerian performance with a 100% uptime SLA. From $35/mo. Read the [Kinsta review](/reviews/kinsta-review).

### 4. Namecheap — best budget with domain
Namecheap starts at ~$1.58/mo and includes a free domain, with PayPal support that's practical for Nigerian users. Performance is middling but the price is the lowest. Read the [Namecheap review](/reviews/namecheap-review).

### 5. Local Nigerian providers
Lagos-based hosts offer **NGN billing, local payment methods, and Nigerian support**. They're the pick when local payment is essential. Performance and reliability vary widely — check uptime records before committing.

## What Nigerian sites should look for

- **Payment flexibility** — PayPal, international cards, or local payment methods
- **UK/EU server** with a CDN for the best balance of speed and reliability
- **Local support** from Nigerian hosts where needed
- **Transparent renewal prices** — the trap that catches most Nigerian buyers

## Frequently asked questions

**What is the best WordPress hosting in Nigeria?**
Hostinger for budget and reliability, Cloudways for managed growth, and Namecheap for the absolute lowest price with a free domain. Local Nigerian hosts if local payment is essential.

**Can I pay for WordPress hosting from Nigeria?**
Most global providers accept international cards and PayPal. Namecheap and Hostinger are the most accessible. Local providers accept NGN and local payment methods.

**Which server region is best for Nigeria?**
UK/EU or South Africa infrastructure with a CDN keeps TTFB around 150–180ms. US-only hosting is noticeably slower.

**Is WordPress hosting expensive in Nigeria?**
No — global providers charge the standard $2–14/mo. Watch renewal jumps, which catch most budget buyers.
