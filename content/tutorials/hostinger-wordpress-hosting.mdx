## Getting started with Hostinger

### Hostinger login
1. Go to **hostinger.com** and click **Log in** (top right).
2. Enter your email and password — or use Google/Facebook single sign-on if you registered that way.
3. You land in the **hPanel dashboard**, where your WordPress websites are listed.

Your username and password are the ones you set at signup. If you used Google login, you'll authenticate through Google instead of typing a password. Forgot your password? The **"Forgot password"** link emails a reset link to your account address.

### Installing WordPress on Hostinger
hPanel has a one-click WordPress installer that provisions a site in 30–60 seconds, including database setup. The exact steps — plus pointing your domain and enabling SSL — are in our [install WordPress on Hostinger](/tutorials/install-wordpress-on-hostinger) tutorial.

### Hostinger wordpress backup
Backups live in **hPanel → Files → Backups**. Daily backups are included on most plans and you can restore a site with one click. For extra safety, enable the free **UpdraftPlus** plugin or the bundled backup add-on.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is Hostinger good for WordPress?**
Yes — it's our top value pick. LiteSpeed + NVMe delivers 142ms TTFB and 99.99% uptime, with a one-click installer and free SSL. Renewal prices jump after the intro term, which is the main caveat.

**How do I log in to Hostinger?**
Visit hostinger.com, click Log in, and enter your account email and password (or your Google/Facebook sign-in). The hPanel dashboard appears immediately after.

**Does Hostinger include a free domain?**
On Premium and Business plans, yes — a free domain for the first year. The cheapest Single plan doesn't include one.

**What is the best Hostinger plan for WordPress?**
Premium Web Hosting. It adds a free domain, unlimited websites, and 100GB NVMe storage for about a dollar more than the entry plan.

**Can I migrate my WordPress site to Hostinger for free?**
Yes. Hostinger's free migration plugin transfers files and database automatically, or their team migrates for you on most plans. See the [migration tutorial](/tutorials/move-wordpress-to-new-host).
