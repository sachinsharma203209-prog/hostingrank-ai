## What a blog actually needs from hosting

Most blogs share the same profile: **small to medium traffic, text and image heavy, monetized with ads or affiliates, and run by one non-technical owner.** That makes the hosting requirements refreshingly simple:

1. **Fast load times** — your bounce rate and ad revenue depend on it
2. **Cheap entry** — blogs don't make money on day one
3. **Free SSL** — required to rank and to display padlocks
4. **Easy WordPress install** — no code, ever
5. **Renewals you can afford** — the intro-price trap hurts blogs most

You don't need enterprise features. You need **reliable shared hosting done well** — the full rankings are in [best WordPress hosting](/best/wordpress-hosting).

## How much should blog hosting cost?

| Stage | What to buy | Monthly cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Starting out | Entry shared (Hostinger Premium) | ~$2.99 |
| Growing traffic | Mid shared with more resources | ~$5–8 |
| High traffic / heavy plugins | Managed cloud or VPS | $14–35 |

A blog under ~30k visits/month does fine on entry shared hosting. Only step up when speed degrades or you're running heavy plugins. If you're monetizing, remember every 100ms of load time costs revenue — our [speed guide](/tutorials/speed-up-wordpress) shows the fixes.

## Frequently asked questions

**What is the best hosting for a new blog?**
Hostinger's Premium plan — $2.99/mo, 142ms TTFB, free SSL, one-click WordPress. For complete beginners, SiteGround's support makes it the low-stress alternative.

**Is cheap hosting good enough for a blog?**
Yes, up to tens of thousands of monthly visits. LiteSpeed + NVMe shared hosting is faster than many bloggers will ever need.

**Do bloggers need managed WordPress hosting?**
No. Managed hosting ($14–35/mo) pays off for revenue-critical business sites, not blogs. A good shared plan is the right cost for blogging.

**How much should I pay for blog hosting?**
$3–6/mo is the realistic range. Watch the renewal price — cheap intros renew higher. See the [budget hosting guide](/best/hosting-under-100).

**How do I start a WordPress blog?**
Pick a host, install WordPress in one click, pick a theme, and write. Our [WordPress install guide](/tutorials/how-to-install-wordpress) covers the first 15 minutes.
