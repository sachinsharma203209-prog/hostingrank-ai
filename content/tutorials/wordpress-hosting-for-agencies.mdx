## What agencies need from WordPress hosting

Agencies run a very different workload than single-site owners. You manage **many client sites**, hand them off to non-technical clients, and carry the blame when something breaks. That changes what "good hosting" means:

- **Multiple sites per account** — no per-site purchasing or billing
- **Staging environments** — test client changes safely
- **Client management tools** — white-label panels, role-based access, and handoff flows
- **Free or easy migrations** — bringing sites over from other hosts
- **Predictable pricing** — ideally flat-rate, not per-visitor surprises
- **Accountable support and SLAs** — for when clients complain

## The best WordPress hosting for agencies

### 2. Cloudways — best flexibility
Cloudways gives agencies **white-label managed WordPress** on any cloud provider, with staging, Redis, and instant scaling — no per-visitor fees. It's our top value pick for agencies running multiple sites. Read the [Cloudways review](/reviews/cloudways-review).

### 3. Kinsta — best premium experience
Kinsta's dashboard is the cleanest we've tested, with per-site teams, staging, and 100% uptime SLAs. Premium pricing from $35/mo. Read the [Kinsta review](/reviews/kinsta-review).

### 4. Hostinger — best budget agency option
Hostinger's unlimited-sites plans and free reseller tools make it the affordable entry for freelancers and small agencies. Read the [Hostinger review](/reviews/hostinger-review).

### 5. Reseller programs
For agencies that want to own the client relationship entirely, reseller plans let you brand and bill your own hosting. See [reseller WordPress hosting](/tutorials/wordpress-reseller-hosting).

## Building your agency hosting stack

The common agency pattern:

1. **Host client sites** on a multi-site managed plan (Cloudways or WP Engine)
2. **Use staging** for every redesign before pushing live
3. **Bundle maintenance** — updates, backups, uptime monitoring — into your retainer. See [WordPress hosting and maintenance](/tutorials/wordpress-hosting-maintenance)
4. **Add reseller margins** for clients who want hosting billed directly

## Frequently asked questions

**What is the best WordPress hosting for agencies?**
WP Engine for enterprise SLAs and stability, Cloudways for value and flexibility, Kinsta for premium managed experience. Hostinger for budget multi-site hosting.

**Can I white-label WordPress hosting for my clients?**
Yes — Cloudways and reseller plans (Hostinger, A2) support white-label branding. WP Engine and Kinsta offer agency programs with partner branding options.

**How many WordPress sites can an agency host on one plan?**
Depends on the plan — Hostinger's unlimited sites hold dozens of small sites; Cloudways hosts many sites per server; WP Engine/Kinsta scale by tier. All are detailed in [WordPress hosting plans](/tutorials/wordpress-hosting-plans).

**Do agencies need managed WordPress hosting?**
If you carry SLAs or client accountability, yes — managed plans (WP Engine, Kinsta, Cloudways) remove update/security risk and give you staging. For freelancers on a budget, Hostinger works.

**How do I migrate client sites to an agency host?**
Use the host's free migration (WP Engine, Kinsta, Cloudways all include it) or a plugin. Our [migration guide](/tutorials/wordpress-hosting-migration) covers both routes.
