## SiteGround WordPress hosting at a glance

SiteGround is the answer to a specific question: *what's the best WordPress host when you value support as much as speed?* Its support team scored **9.4/10** in our tests — the best of any provider we benchmarked — and its Google Cloud infrastructure delivers a **185ms TTFB**, which is excellent for shared hosting.

- **Starting price:** $3.99/mo (billed annually)
- **Panel:** custom Site Tools
- **Freebies:** free SSL, daily backups, free CDN, free migration
- **Data centers:** 11 global, including US, UK, EU, and Asia
- **Guarantee:** 30-day money-back

Read the full [SiteGround review](/reviews/siteground-review).

## SiteGround WordPress hosting plans

| Plan | Price | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| StartUp | $3.99/mo | 1 site, 10GB, ~10k visits/mo |
| GrowBig | $6.99/mo | Multiple sites, more resources |
| GoGeek | $10.99/mo | Devs: staging, Git, more CPU |

Renewal pricing jumps noticeably after the intro term — budget for ~$15/mo on StartUp at renewal. The GrowBig tier is the best value if you run more than one site or expect real traffic.

## What SiteGround does well

- **Best-in-class support** — 24/7 chat and phone, fast median responses, and agents who actually fix WordPress issues
- **Google Cloud infrastructure** — reliable, with excellent latency across 11 regions
- **SG Optimizer plugin** — handles caching, lazy loading, and more without extra plugins
- **Daily backups** — included, not a paid add-on
- **Free migration** — their team moves your site for free

## Where it falls short

- **Renewal price jump** — from $3.99 to ~$15/mo after year one
- **Entry plan limits** — StartUp caps you at 10GB storage and roughly 10k visits/mo
- **No unlimited anything** — resource caps are explicit and enforced

## Frequently asked questions

**Is SiteGround good for WordPress?**
Yes — it's our top pick for beginners who want a managed experience without premium prices. Google Cloud infrastructure, 185ms TTFB, and the best support in the industry.

**What is the best SiteGround WordPress plan?**
GrowBig for most users — multiple sites, 20GB storage, and staging. GoGeek if you're a developer who needs Git and more CPU. StartUp only if you'll never grow past one small site.

**Is SiteGround expensive after the first year?**
Renewal prices run roughly 3–4x the intro rate. Budget for ~$15/mo (StartUp) at renewal — still reasonable for what you get, but not the "$4/mo" you saw at signup.

**Does SiteGround include WordPress hosting and email?**
Yes — Site Tools includes email accounts on all plans, and daily backups plus a free CDN are included.

**SiteGround or Cloudways for WordPress?**
SiteGround for a single site with top support; Cloudways for VPS performance, scaling, and developer features. Full data in the [Cloudways vs SiteGround](/comparisons/cloudways-vs-siteground) guide.
