## A2 Hosting WordPress at a glance

A2 Hosting is the developer-friendly budget host best known for its **Turbo plans** — LiteSpeed + NVMe shared hosting that performs like a VPS. Our tests measured a **210ms TTFB** on Turbo, with an **anytime money-back guarantee** that's the best risk-reversal in the industry.

- **Starting price:** $2.99/mo (billed over 36 months)
- **TTFB (our tests):** 210ms
- **Panel:** cPanel
- **Freebies:** free SSL, free site migration
- **Guarantee:** anytime money-back

## What A2 does well

- **Turbo speed** — LiteSpeed + NVMe delivers 210ms TTFB, VPS-like for shared
- **Anytime money-back** — unique; no other major host offers this
- **Developer tools** — SSH, staging (on some plans), Git, and PHP version control
- **Free migration** — their team moves your site for free
- **24/7 Guru Crew support** — genuinely knowledgeable support staff

## Where it falls short

- **Renewal prices** climb after the intro term (Turbo plans jump significantly)
- **Entry plans are resource-lean** — 1 site, limited CPU on Startup
- **Phone support** hours are limited

## Frequently asked questions

**Is A2 Hosting good for WordPress?**
Yes — the Turbo plans combine LiteSpeed + NVMe for 210ms TTFB at budget prices, with an anytime money-back guarantee. One of the best budget-to-performance ratios we've tested.

**What is the best A2 Hosting plan for WordPress?**
Turbo Boost — LiteSpeed and NVMe with unlimited sites. Non-Turbo plans are fine but don't justify A2 over cheaper competitors.

**What is A2's anytime money-back guarantee?**
A2 lets you cancel and get a prorated refund at any time — not just within 30 days. It's the strongest guarantee in the industry.

**Does A2 Hosting include cPanel?**
Yes — cPanel across all plans, plus SSH and developer-friendly features on higher tiers.

**A2 Hosting or Hostinger?**
Hostinger is faster (142ms vs 210ms) and cheaper; A2 wins on cPanel, the anytime guarantee, and stronger developer tooling. Both are covered in [cheap WordPress hosting](/tutorials/cheap-wordpress-hosting).
