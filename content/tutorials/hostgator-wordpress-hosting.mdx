## HostGator WordPress hosting at a glance

HostGator is one of the biggest budget names in hosting, with a huge footprint in India and the US. Its pitch is simple: **the lowest entry price** with familiar cPanel. Our tests confirm the price — and also confirm the tradeoffs.

- **Starting price:** $2.75/mo (billed over 36 months)
- **TTFB (our tests):** 410ms — slower than LiteSpeed rivals
- **Panel:** cPanel
- **Uptime:** 99.95%
- **Guarantee:** 45-day money-back

Read the full [HostGator review](/reviews/hostgator-review).

## HostGator WordPress hosting plans

| Plan | Price | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hatchling | $2.75/mo | 1 site |
| Baby | $3.50/mo | Unlimited sites |
| Business | $5.25/mo | Adds SEO tools, backups |

All plans include unmetered bandwidth, free SSL, and cPanel. The **Baby** plan is the value pick. Renewal pricing jumps significantly after the intro term — Hatchling renews well above its teaser price.

## What HostGator does well

- **Lowest entry prices** — among the cheapest ways into hosting
- **Unmetered bandwidth** on most plans
- **Familiar cPanel** — every tutorial in existence applies
- **45-day money-back guarantee** — one of the longest
- **Indian presence** — HostGator India accepts INR with local support

## Where it falls short

- **Performance** — 410ms TTFB in our India tests; the oversold shared servers show noisy-neighbor slowdowns at peak hours
- **Renewal jumps** — aggressive pricing after year one
- **Upsells** — site builder and extras pushed hard at checkout
- **Single-tier tech** — Apache-based stack rather than LiteSpeed/NVMe

## Frequently asked questions

**Is HostGator good for WordPress?**
It's an adequate budget option. The price and cPanel are familiar, but 410ms TTFB and aggressive renewals make faster hosts like Hostinger a better buy for most sites.

**What is the best HostGator plan for WordPress?**
The Baby plan — unlimited sites and unmetered bandwidth for a few dollars more than Hatchling. Avoid the add-on-heavy Business tier unless you specifically want its bundled tools.

**Is HostGator WordPress hosting fast?**
Our tests measured 410ms median TTFB — usable, but noticeably slower than LiteSpeed + NVMe competitors at the same price.

**Does HostGator include free WordPress hosting tools?**
Yes — free SSL, a one-click WordPress installer in cPanel, and unmetered bandwidth. Email is available on all plans.

**What is the best cheap WordPress hosting instead of HostGator?**
Hostinger — same price, 3x faster. See [cheap WordPress hosting](/tutorials/cheap-wordpress-hosting) for the comparison.
