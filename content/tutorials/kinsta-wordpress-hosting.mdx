## Kinsta WordPress hosting at a glance

Kinsta is the performance king of managed WordPress hosting. Across our six-month test it held a **98ms global TTFB** — the fastest of any WordPress host we measured — with a **perfect uptime record** and support responses under two minutes. You pay for that: plans start at **$35/mo**.

- **Starting price:** $35/mo (billed monthly)
- **Infrastructure:** Google Cloud + Cloudflare edge caching
- **Freebies:** free migration, free SSL, staging, CDN
- **Uptime SLA:** 100%
- **Panel:** custom MyKinsta dashboard

Read the full [Kinsta review](/reviews/kinsta-review).

## Kinsta WordPress hosting plans

| Plan | Price | Visitors/mo | Storage |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Starter | $35/mo | 25k | 10GB |
| Pro | $70/mo | 50k | 20GB |
| Business 1 | $115/mo | 100k | 30GB |
| Business 4 | $230/mo | 400k | 60GB |
| Agency 1+ | $400+/mo | 600k+ | 100GB+ |

All plans include the same core stack — the differences are visitors, storage, and support priority. The visitor-based pricing is worth understanding: exceed your monthly visits and you'll either pay per extra 1,000 visitors or upgrade.

## What Kinsta does exceptionally well

- **Speed** — 98ms TTFB and 0.6s load time in our tests; Cloudflare edge caching included on every plan
- **Reliability** — 100% uptime across six months, backed by a formal SLA
- **Support** — expert WordPress team, sub-2-minute median responses, genuinely helpful
- **Security** — free SSL, uptime monitoring, daily backups, and malware scanning
- **Developer tools** — staging, Git-based deploys, WP-CLI, and a first-class API
- **Free migration** — their team handles it for you

## Where it falls short

- **Price** — $35/mo entry is 10x the cost of good shared hosting
- **Visitor limits** — strict monthly visitor caps on every tier
- **No email hosting** — you'll need a separate service (many hosts include this)
- **No phone support** — chat and tickets only

## Is Kinsta worth it?

The honest answer depends on your site:

- **Revenue-critical site?** Yes — the uptime, speed, and support pay for themselves after one avoided outage.
- **Agency running client sites?** Yes — the dashboard, staging, and migration tooling are best-in-class.
- **Blog or small business on a budget?** No — [Hostinger](/reviews/hostinger-review) at $2.99/mo delivers 142ms TTFB, and [Cloudways](/reviews/cloudways-review) gives you most of Kinsta's flexibility at a third of the price.

The [Kinsta vs Cloudways](/comparisons/kinsta-vs-cloudways) comparison has the full data — Cloudways posts nearly identical TTFB for a fraction of the cost.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is Kinsta good for WordPress?**
It's the best-performing WordPress host we've tested — 98ms TTFB, 100% uptime, and exceptional support. The only question is whether premium pricing fits your site.

**How much does Kinsta cost?**
$35/mo for the Starter plan (25k visitors, 10GB storage). Business plans run $115–230/mo; agency plans start around $400/mo.

**Does Kinsta host multiple WordPress sites?**
Every plan above Starter includes multiple sites, and you can add more. Agency plans are built for high site counts.

**Is Kinsta worth the money vs cheaper WordPress hosting?**
For revenue-critical or agency sites, yes — uptime, speed, and support justify the price. For hobby or small sites, Hostinger or Cloudways are better value.

**How do I migrate my WordPress site to Kinsta?**
Kinsta migrates your site for free. Just open a migration request in MyKinsta and their team handles it. Our [move WordPress to a new host](/tutorials/move-wordpress-to-new-host) guide covers the general process.
