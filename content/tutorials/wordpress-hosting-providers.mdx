## WordPress hosting companies vs platforms

Beyond traditional hosts, there's a second category: **platforms** that host WordPress differently.

- **Vercel** and **Cloudflare Pages** — blazing-fast edge delivery (28–32ms TTFB) but no managed WordPress — you'd static-export WordPress. See [best hosting for Next.js](/best/hosting-for-nextjs) for the platform comparison.
- **AWS, Azure, Google Cloud** — raw cloud infrastructure. Hosting WordPress on [AWS](/tutorials/wordpress-hosting-aws) gives you full control but no support for the WordPress layer.
- **Cloudflare / Bunny** — CDN services that speed up any WordPress host from the edge.

## Frequently asked questions

**Which WordPress hosting provider is best?**
For most people, Hostinger — best balance of speed, price, and features. Kinsta is the premium performance pick, SiteGround the best for support.

**What is the most reliable WordPress hosting provider?**
Kinsta (100% uptime in our six-month test) and Hostinger (99.99%) were the most reliable. WP Engine and Cloudways were close behind.

**How many WordPress hosting providers are there?**
Hundreds of resellers, but the market is effectively dominated by about 15–20 infrastructure providers. The ten above cover every use case and budget.

**Are cheap WordPress hosting providers safe?**
The reputable ones (Hostinger, Namecheap, HostGator) include free SSL, daily backups, and account isolation. Avoid unknown cheap hosts that promise "unlimited everything" — that's the classic overselling red flag.
