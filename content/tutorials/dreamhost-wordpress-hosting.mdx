## DreamHost WordPress hosting at a glance

DreamHost is the independent, privacy-minded host with the longest guarantee in the business — **97 days** — and one of the most user-friendly custom panels we've used. It's officially recommended by WordPress.org and has been running since 1996.

- **Starting price:** $2.59/mo (billed over 36 months)
- **TTFB (our tests):** 290ms
- **Panel:** custom DreamHost panel
- **Freebies:** free SSL, automated WordPress updates, free domain (annual plans)
- **Guarantee:** 97-day money-back

Read the full [DreamHost review](/reviews/dreamhost-review).

The **Shared Unlimited** plan is the value pick. **DreamPress** is their managed WordPress product — a solid middle ground between shared and premium managed (see [managed WordPress hosting](/tutorials/managed-wordpress-hosting)).

## What DreamHost does well

- **97-day guarantee** — by far the longest trial window; essentially a quarter to test
- **Automatic WordPress updates** — core, theme, and plugin updates handled
- **Clean custom panel** — no cPanel clutter, genuinely easy to navigate
- **Privacy-minded** — independent company, strong privacy stance
- **Free domain** on annual plans, plus free SSL

## Where it falls short

- **Performance** — 290ms TTFB trails LiteSpeed competitors
- **No cPanel** — the custom panel surprises users who know cPanel
- **Chat support is email-based** — slower than chat-first hosts
- **US-based infrastructure** — weaker for international audiences

## Frequently asked questions

**Is DreamHost good for WordPress?**
Yes — WordPress.org-recommended, automatic updates, and a 97-day guarantee make it low-risk. Performance (290ms TTFB) is solid but not class-leading.

**What is DreamHost's money-back guarantee?**
97 days on shared plans — the longest in the industry. Cancel within that window for a full refund.

**Does DreamHost include automatic WordPress updates?**
Yes — core, plugin, and theme updates are handled automatically, which is one of its strongest beginner features.

**Does DreamHost have cPanel?**
No — DreamHost uses its own custom panel. It's cleaner and easier for beginners, but not cPanel.

**DreamHost or Hostinger for WordPress?**
DreamHost for the guarantee and automatic updates; Hostinger for speed and price. Full reviews: [DreamHost](/reviews/dreamhost-review) and [Hostinger](/reviews/hostinger-review).
