## WP Engine WordPress hosting at a glance

WP Engine is the enterprise-grade managed WordPress host. Where Kinsta wins on raw performance, WP Engine wins on **maturity and stability for agencies**: enterprise SLAs, a 60-day money-back guarantee, and a plugin review process that keeps client sites predictable.

- **Starting price:** $20/mo (billed annually)
- **Stack:** purpose-built WordPress with EverCache
- **Freebies:** free migration, SSL, staging, CDN, Genesis theme
- **Guarantee:** 60-day money-back
- **Uptime SLA:** 99.95%

Like Kinsta, WP Engine uses **visitor-based pricing** — plan tiers scale by monthly visits rather than just storage. A 60-day guarantee means you can test it thoroughly before committing.

## What WP Engine does well

- **Enterprise SLAs** — a real uptime guarantee with service credits, rare in WordPress hosting
- **EverCache** — their caching layer keeps high-traffic sites fast (170ms TTFB in our tests)
- **Stability for agencies** — a vetted plugin list and automatic backups protect client work
- **Developer platform** — staging, Git integration, WP-CLI, and a strong API
- **Genesis framework** — premium themes included free
- **60-day guarantee** — the longest test window of any major host

## Where it falls short

- **Visitor caps** — every plan has strict monthly visit limits; exceeding them costs extra
- **No email hosting** — bundled email is not included
- **Plugin restrictions** — some plugins are blocked to protect the server (rarely an issue, but it exists)
- **Premium price** — from $20/mo, comparable to Kinsta

## Frequently asked questions

**Is WP Engine good for WordPress?**
Yes — it's our top recommendation for agencies and enterprises. Enterprise SLAs, EverCache, and a 60-day guarantee make it the most dependable managed WordPress option.

**How much does WP Engine cost?**
From $20/mo for a single site (25k visits), $40/mo for 3 sites, and up through agency tiers. Visitor-based pricing means heavy sites pay more.

**What is EverCache in WP Engine?**
WP Engine's proprietary caching layer, applied across page cache, object cache, and CDN. It's why WP Engine sustains 170ms TTFB under load.

**Does WP Engine include email hosting?**
No — email is not bundled. Use the host's recommended providers or a separate service.

**WP Engine or Kinsta?**
Kinsta for raw performance and 100% uptime; WP Engine for enterprise stability, SLAs, and the 60-day guarantee. Both are premium — see the [managed WordPress hosting](/tutorials/managed-wordpress-hosting) guide for the full comparison.
