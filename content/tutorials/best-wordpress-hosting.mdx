## The short version

We tested 40+ WordPress hosting providers for six months, tracking real **TTFB**, uptime under load, support response times, and renewal pricing. Here are the winners:

1. **Hostinger** — the best value WordPress hosting overall (142ms TTFB, $2.99/mo)
2. **Kinsta** — the fastest and most polished managed experience (98ms TTFB, 100% uptime)
3. **Cloudways** — the best managed cloud for developers and agencies
4. **SiteGround** — the best support for WordPress beginners
5. **WP Engine** — the best enterprise option for agencies

Full data and rankings live in our [best WordPress hosting](/best/wordpress-hosting) hub. Below is the how-and-why behind each pick.

## How we test WordPress hosting

Every provider in our rankings runs the same WordPress test site from the same 12 global test nodes. We record the **median** of three runs for TTFB, load time, and LCP, then stress-test uptime for 30 days. Renewal pricing is read from the live checkout, not the marketing page. Our full [methodology](/methodology) is public.

## The top WordPress hosting picks

### 1. Hostinger — best overall value
Hostinger's LiteSpeed + NVMe stack is the performance-per-dollar outlier of the market. In our tests it held a **142ms global TTFB** and **99.99% uptime**, at an intro price of $2.99/mo. It includes a free SSL, daily backups, a one-click WordPress installer, and an easy hPanel. The main catches: renewal prices jump after the intro term, and there's no phone support. Read the full [Hostinger review](/reviews/hostinger-review).

### 2. Kinsta — fastest managed WordPress
Kinsta runs WordPress on Google Cloud with Cloudflare edge caching included. It posted **98ms TTFB** and a perfect uptime record in our test window, with support that answered in under two minutes. You pay for it — plans start at $35/mo — but for a revenue-critical site it's worth every rupee. Read the [Kinsta review](/reviews/kinsta-review).

### 3. Cloudways — best for developers
Cloudways gives you a managed layer on top of AWS, Google Cloud, DigitalOcean, or Vultr. That means staging environments, Redis, and a clean dashboard without managing a server. Its 124ms TTFB was the fastest non-Kinsta result we measured. Read the [Cloudways review](/reviews/cloudways-review).

### 4. SiteGround — best beginner support
SiteGround pairs Google Cloud infrastructure with the friendliest support team in the industry (9.4/10 in our tests). TTFB of 185ms is excellent for shared hosting, and their SG Optimizer plugin handles caching for you. Read the [SiteGround review](/reviews/siteground-review).

### 5. WP Engine — best for agencies
WP Engine is built for agencies running multiple revenue-critical WordPress sites, with enterprise SLAs, EverCache, and a 60-day money-back guarantee. Read the [WP Engine review](/reviews/wp-engine-review).
