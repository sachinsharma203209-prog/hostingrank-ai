## Is free WordPress hosting actually free?

Yes — but "free" always comes with strings. Free WordPress hosting plans give you a working WordPress site with zero upfront cost, funded by one or more of these tradeoffs:

- **Forced ads** on your site
- **Hard storage and bandwidth caps** (often 1GB or less)
- **No email hosting** and no custom domain on many plans
- **Unacceptable performance** under any real traffic
- **No uptime guarantee** and slow support

For a personal project, portfolio, or learning WordPress, free hosting is a legitimate option. For a business site, blog you care about, or any site you want to rank in Google, a $3/mo plan is a dramatically better investment.

## The types of free WordPress hosting

### 1. Hosted platforms (WordPress.com free)
WordPress.com's free plan lets you build a blog on their infrastructure. You get a `yourname.wordpress.com` subdomain, ads on your site, 1GB of storage, and you can't install plugins or custom themes. It's a free WordPress website, not really WordPress hosting you control.

### 2. Free tiers of commercial hosts
Some providers advertise free trials — usually 30 days — and free plans with very low limits. These are best used to test a host before committing, not as permanent homes.

### 3. Open-source + free infrastructure
You can run WordPress for free on cloud free tiers like **AWS Free Tier**, **Google Cloud Free Tier**, or **Oracle Cloud's Always Free** VPS. These are genuinely free for 12 months (or always on Oracle), but you must configure, secure, and update everything yourself. ## What free WordPress hosting limits look like

A typical free plan offers:

- 1GB storage (about 3,000 photos or a small blog)
- 10–50GB monthly bandwidth
- No custom domain (or a subdomain only)
- No email accounts
- No daily backups
- No staging environment

## When free WordPress hosting makes sense

- Learning WordPress or testing themes/plugins
- A temporary landing page or portfolio
- A dev/staging copy of a real site
- Proof-of-concept before buying a plan

## When it doesn't

- Any site you plan to monetize or rank in search
- E-commerce or anything handling customer data
- A client website with a professional reputation on the line

A free host that's down when a customer visits costs far more than $3/mo saved.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is free WordPress hosting safe?**
Risky. Free hosts rarely offer backups, malware scanning, or guaranteed uptime. If you must use one, back your site up manually and never store sensitive data on it.

**What is the best free WordPress hosting?**
For a real, permanent site there isn't one we can recommend with confidence. For testing, use a provider's 30-day free trial — you get production-grade performance without paying. For truly free infrastructure with full control, the AWS free tier and Oracle Always Free VPS are the technical options.

**Can I host WordPress for free with a free domain?**
Yes, with a subdomain like `mysite.wordpress.com` or `mysite.vercel.app`. A real custom domain costs roughly $10/yr and is worth it for credibility and email.

**Does free WordPress hosting include email?**
Almost never. You'll need a separate email service, which makes "free" less free in practice.
