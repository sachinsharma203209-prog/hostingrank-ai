## The two philosophies

WordPress and Webflow represent two very different bets on how to build a website:

- **WordPress + hosting** — open-source software on hosting you own. Unlimited extension, full data ownership.
- **Webflow** — a visual design tool with hosting included. Stunning output, but you rent the platform.

This is the same decision as [WordPress vs site builders](/tutorials/wordpress-hosting-vs-site-builders) with a design twist: Webflow is the builder that designers love, because its visual canvas is genuinely excellent.

## Cost compared

| | WordPress + hosting | Webflow |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Entry | $2.99/mo hosting + ~$10/yr domain | $14/mo (Basic, annual) or $19/mo |
| CMS sites | $5/mo hosting is plenty | $23/mo (CMS plan) |
| Design | Free/paid themes; page builders | Built into the visual editor |
| Ongoing | ~$3–8/mo all-in | $14–35/mo forever |

Webflow bills **per site** — a portfolio on the CMS plan costs $23/mo. A WordPress site of any size runs on $3–8/mo hosting. Over three years: WordPress ~$200, Webflow ~$800+. The numbers are covered in [cheap WordPress hosting](/tutorials/cheap-wordpress-hosting).

## Design: where Webflow genuinely wins

Webflow's design surface is the best in the business — pixel-perfect, animation-friendly, and it exports clean HTML/CSS. If your site's entire value is bespoke design (a launch page, a creative portfolio), Webflow is a real advantage.

WordPress matches it with page builders (Elementor, Bricks) but the result is heavier and the workflow is less fluid. The trade: WordPress design freedom is *possible*, Webflow's is *native*.

## Performance and SEO

- **WordPress:** speed is yours to control — with the right [hosting](/best/wordpress-hosting) you hit sub-200ms TTFB. Full SEO control.
- **Webflow:** clean, fast-by-default output on Cloudflare infrastructure. Solid basics, but you can't tune the server, and complex setups cost more.

For SEO-heavy content sites, WordPress remains stronger: schema, redirects, and technical control are all yours. See [WordPress hosting and SEO](/tutorials/wordpress-hosting-and-seo).

## Lock-in: the real difference

**WordPress** — you own the files, database, and content. Switch hosts anytime via a [migration](/tutorials/wordpress-hosting-migration), or move to another CMS.

**Webflow** — your site lives inside Webflow. Export is a static HTML snapshot: no CMS content model, no dynamic pages, no plugins. If Webflow raises prices or you outgrow it, you rebuild.

## The verdict

**Choose WordPress + hosting if:**
- You want ownership, low cost, and no platform lock-in
- You need plugins, ecommerce depth, or heavy SEO
- You're building a business or content site with growth plans

**Choose Webflow if:**
- Design is the product and you want the best visual editor available
- You're okay paying per site and being locked to the platform
- You're a designer or studio delivering launch sites and marketing pages

For a marketing site with brilliant design as the deliverable, Webflow is defensible. For anything that needs to grow, take revenue, or last a decade, **WordPress hosting is the cheaper, more flexible, more portable answer**.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is Webflow cheaper than WordPress?**
No — Webflow starts at $14/mo and $23/mo for CMS sites, forever, per site. WordPress hosting is $3–8/mo with full ownership.

**Is Webflow better than WordPress for design?**
For pure visual design, yes — Webflow's editor is the best. WordPress catches up with page builders but with a heavier result.

**Can I move from Webflow to WordPress?**
You can export static HTML and import content, but the dynamic structure, CMS, and design don't transfer cleanly. Budget a rebuild. Our [migration guide](/tutorials/wordpress-hosting-migration) covers the move.

**Is Webflow good for SEO?**
Good basics — clean output, decent speed. But WordPress offers deeper technical control (schema, redirects, server tuning). See [WordPress hosting and SEO](/tutorials/wordpress-hosting-and-seo).

**Who should use Webflow instead of WordPress?**
Designers and studios delivering visually-driven marketing sites where design is the deliverable and per-site pricing is acceptable. Everyone else: [best WordPress hosting](/best/wordpress-hosting).
