## Bluehost WordPress hosting at a glance

Bluehost is one of the few hosts **officially recommended by WordPress.org**, and for a specific kind of user it earns that spot: WordPress beginners who want guided onboarding, a free domain, and one dashboard for everything. But "recommended" is not "fastest."

Our six-month test measured a **680ms TTFB** from Bluehost's US-East infrastructure — roughly **2–4x slower** than modern LiteSpeed + NVMe hosts at the same price. For a site you plan to grow and rank, that's the number to think about.

- **Starting price:** $2.95/mo (billed over 36 months)
- **Panel:** cPanel with a WordPress-first dashboard
- **Freebies:** free domain, free SSL, one-click WordPress install
- **Data centers:** US (single primary region)
- **Guarantee:** 30-day money-back

## What Bluehost does well

- **WordPress.org official recommendation** — genuine trust signal for beginners
- **Guided onboarding** — the setup wizard genuinely helps first-timers
- **Free domain + SSL** — included on most plans
- **cPanel familiarity** — thousands of tutorials exist for anything you'd want to do

## Where it falls short

- **Performance** — 680ms TTFB trails LiteSpeed hosts by 2–4x
- **Single data center** — one primary US region; weak international performance
- **Aggressive upsells** — site builder, SEO tools, and more at checkout
- **Resource limits** — entry plans cap CPU/RAM despite "unlimited" marketing

## Frequently asked questions

**Is Bluehost good for WordPress?**
For absolute beginners, yes — the guided onboarding, free domain, and WordPress.org endorsement are real. For performance, no — 680ms TTFB is 2–4x slower than LiteSpeed competitors at the same price.

**What is the best Bluehost plan for WordPress?**
The Plus plan — unlimited sites and storage for ~$5/mo intro. Avoid the Basic if you might add a second site; migrating plans later is fiddly.

**Is Bluehost WordPress hosting worth it in 2026?**
Only if you value the beginner hand-holding more than speed. If TTFB matters for SEO or conversions, Hostinger or SiteGround deliver far better performance for similar money.

**How do I get a Bluehost WordPress hosting login?**
Bluehost sends your cPanel login at signup. Use the **Log in** button on bluehost.com with your account email and password — your WordPress admin is at `/wp-admin`.

**Can I migrate my WordPress site to Bluehost?**
Yes, Bluehost includes free migration. If you're moving the other direction, see our [move WordPress to a new host](/tutorials/move-wordpress-to-new-host) guide.
