## What a portfolio needs from hosting

A portfolio is an image-heavy, design-driven site that must load fast enough to make a first impression. Its hosting needs are specific:

1. **Speed on image-heavy pages** — heavy galleries punish slow servers
2. **Reliability** — a portfolio that's down when a client looks is a lost opportunity
3. **Enough storage** — 1080p+ work samples eat space fast
4. **A professional look** — a custom domain and fast load times are part of your brand

The good news: portfolios rarely have heavy traffic or complex plugins, so **budget hosting done well is plenty** — spend the savings on your work, not the server.

## Portfolio-specific speed tips

- **Compress images** — a portfolio's #1 speed killer is uncompressed images. Convert to WebP and lazy-load galleries. Full guide: [speed up WordPress](/tutorials/speed-up-wordpress)
- **Add a CDN** — for galleries, a [CDN](/tutorials/wordpress-hosting-cdn) serves your images from the edge, which matters for remote clients
- **Pick a lean theme** — portfolio themes with bloated builders defeat fast hosting
- **Enable caching** — LiteSpeed Cache on LiteSpeed hosts or a standard cache plugin

## Storage: the hidden requirement

Portfolio work is storage-hungry — 100 high-res samples at 5MB each is 500MB before anything else. Check your plan's storage:

- **Hostinger Premium:** 100GB NVMe — ample
- **Namecheap:** 20–50GB — fine for most
- **Cloudways:** 50GB+ per server — plenty for video-heavy work

If your work includes video reels, consider hosting video on YouTube/Vimeo embeds rather than your server — it keeps your hosting plan small and fast.

## Frequently asked questions

**What is the best cheap hosting for a portfolio?**
Hostinger at $2.99/mo — fast on image-heavy pages, 100GB NVMe storage. Namecheap is the cheaper alternative if you want the free domain.

**Does a portfolio need managed WordPress hosting?**
No — portfolios have light traffic and few plugins, so shared hosting is the right cost. Managed hosting only pays off for revenue-critical sites.

**How much storage does a portfolio need?**
50–100GB covers most photographers and designers. Video-heavy portfolios should embed from Vimeo/YouTube to keep hosting lean.

**How do I make my portfolio load fast?**
Compress images, enable caching, and add a CDN. Run the [speed guide](/tutorials/speed-up-wordpress) after launch — portfolios live or die on load time.

**Do portfolio sites need a professional email?**
Yes — clients email the address on your site. A custom domain mailbox is free with most hosting. [Email setup](/tutorials/wordpress-email-setup).
