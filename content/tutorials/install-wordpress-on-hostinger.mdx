## Why install WordPress on Hostinger?

Hostinger's one-click WordPress installer is the fastest way to launch a site. The whole setup takes about **15 minutes**, including domain connection and SSL, and requires zero command-line experience.

Because Hostinger uses NVMe storage and LiteSpeed on most plans, a fresh WordPress install typically boots with a **TTFB under 200ms** in India and ~360ms globally in our probes.

## What you'll need

- A Hostinger plan (we recommend at least Premium Web for the free domain and NVMe)
- A domain name (free first year on Premium plans)
- 15 minutes

## Step-by-step

Follow the six steps above from your hPanel dashboard. The only step that can trip people up is **pointing the domain**, so here's the detail:

### Pointing your domain to Hostinger

1. In hPanel, open **Websites → your site → Domains**.
2. Choose **Add Domain**, type your domain, and hit confirm.
3. At your registrar (wherever you bought the domain), change the nameservers to Hostinger's:
   - `ns1.dns-parking.com`
   - `ns2.dns-parking.com`
4. DNS propagates within 24 hours (usually 1-4 hours). hPanel's **Domain Checker** shows live status.

### Verify HTTPS

After propagation, confirm the padlock in your browser. If HTTPS isn't active:

1. Open hPanel → **Security → SSL**.
2. Click **Install** next to your domain (Let's Encrypt, free).
3. Toggle **Force HTTPS** so all traffic upgrades automatically.
