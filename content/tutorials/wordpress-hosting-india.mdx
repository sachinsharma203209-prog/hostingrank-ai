## WordPress hosting in India, measured

If your audience is in India, the fastest way to load your WordPress site is a server in India. Our tests show a **Mumbai or Bangalore data center cuts TTFB by ~60%** compared to a US or EU server — typically from 200–400ms down to **30–60ms**.

Local hosting also means **INR pricing with UPI, RuPay, and NetBanking**, no forex markup, and support in local business hours. The best WordPress hosting in India combines all three.

## Should you host WordPress on a local India server?

**Yes if:** your visitors are mostly in India and you want the fastest possible load for them.

**Not necessarily if:** your audience is global. A CDN on top of a good server matters more than server location for international traffic.

Note that some Indian providers route from Singapore rather than Mumbai — check the data center before buying. The difference is obvious in the latency figures above.

## How to pay in INR with UPI
